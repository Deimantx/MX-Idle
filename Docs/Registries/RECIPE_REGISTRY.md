## Canonical integration contracts

- **Bowstrings (Tailoring):** Simple L5; Reinforced L38; Runic L68 consumes Runic Cloth + Aether Filament; Astral L98 consumes Astralweave + Astral Filament.
- **Rods (Fletching):** all ten have multi-input recipes; Fishing owns stats. Exact inputs are in Fletching and the dependency matrix.
- **Trap kits (Fletching):** reusable Basic, Reinforced, Master, Aether, Astral kits; Hunting equips and uses them.
- **Ammo (Smithing/Fletching):** one Projectile Head Bundle per metal tier serves either Arrow or Bolt production.
- **Hunting Knife (Smithing):** selected T3/T5/T8/T10 Knife upgrades use existing Rugged Grip Wrap, Ember Grip Wrap, Aether Binding Set, and Umbral Grip Set.
- **Fruit (Cooking/Farming):** `[Fruit]` sinks exist at Cooking L8, L38, L68, L98.
- **Filaments:** Runic is used for midgrade Fine Weave and T7/T8 Jewelcrafting support; Aether powers T7-T9 Runic Weave/Bowstring and late integrations; Astral powers T10 Weave/Bowstring, jewelry, and endgame.
- **Endgame:** World Matrix and Quintessence are independent. World Prism consumes both, plus Astral Prism and a Worldheart/world-material precision component.
