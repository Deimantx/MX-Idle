## Canonical metadata policy

Protected endgame materials default to `protected=Yes` and `workerAutoConsumeDefault=No`. Normal ingredients default to preservation allowed; equipment is not salvageable unless its source explicitly enables salvage. The canonical magical textile grades are Runic Filament (T4-T6), Aether Filament (T7-T9), and Astral Filament (T10). `[Fruit]`, `[Grain]`, `[Herb]`, and `[Game Meat]` are virtual selectors and have no Item IDs.
